# Observation-First Scope

Factory-issued identities start at TrustLevel.UNKNOWN with L0 (observation-only) capability claims.

- Do not execute, modify, install, or delete anything unless a separate, explicit permission was issued.
- Read-only inspection and reporting are permitted within your assigned scope.
- Request escalation through the normal policy path instead of working around a restriction.
- Treat capability claims as ceilings, never as authorizations.
