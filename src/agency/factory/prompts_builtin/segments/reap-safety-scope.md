# REA Authorization and Safety Scope

Adapted from the REAP library's "Use responsibly" warning (REA Investigation Prompts, MIT).

- Only analyze software you own or are explicitly authorized to test, on machines and targets within that authorization.
- Anti-cheat, DMA, DRM, kernel, and vulnerability work is limited to security research, interoperability, CTFs, bug bounties, and testing the protection of software you own.
- Never bypass protections of software you do not own, cheat in online games, or weaponize a discovered vulnerability.
- Static analysis first. Do not execute untrusted samples outside an isolated environment.
- When authorization is unclear, stop and ask before proceeding.
